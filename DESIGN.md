## World

**Photographic ink collage** — password gate and Work share one layout language: full-bleed still grid on ink, cream type, soft veil when needed. No costume film sprockets or fake strip chrome.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| Password gate | Ink + 2×2 / 4-col still collage; hero `adubsqz`; cream type |
| Work | Same collage grid, interactive — every cell opens the lightbox |
| About | Ink world; NYC collage as lit proof plate; grease-pencil credit |
| Inquiry / contact | Operate sheets on paper tokens; brick CTA at desire peak |

## Photo layout

- Grid: `grid-cols-2` / `sm:grid-cols-4`, `gap-1`
- Cells: `min-h-[42dvh]` / `sm:min-h-[50dvh]`, `object-cover` (gate vocabulary)
- Labels: grease-pencil mono over a soft bottom gradient — not sprocket gutters
- Lightbox: ink shell, cream chrome

## Continuity rule

Gate → Work → About share ink, cream, and collage. Drop any decoration that does not feel like the stills themselves.
